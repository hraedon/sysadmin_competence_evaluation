CERTIFICATE MANAGEMENT PROCESS NOTES
------------------------------------
1. Certificate requests are logged in CertTracker_v3.xlsx.
2. Security team updates the tracker manually after installation.
3. CertWatch reads the spreadsheet daily and alerts 14 days before expiry.
4. Renewal requests are opened via ServiceNow at 10 days before expiry.
5. Load balancer certificates are updated by the Network team from a
   separate certificate store on F5; tracker row is updated manually
   by Security after Network confirms deployment.
6. Root and intermediate CA trust chains are maintained in AD DS Group
   Policy and are not reviewed during certificate expiry monitoring.
