There's a problem with this backup job that isn't visible from the success status alone.

The job is configured to keep 7 restore points, but only 3 are available: the full from 2026-03-16 and incrementals from 2026-03-17 and 2026-03-18. Seven consecutive job runs have completed with Success status, yet the restore point count has not increased. There are no restore points for 2026-03-19 through 2026-03-22 — if someone needs to recover a file deleted on any of those four days, there is nothing to recover from.

The repository is at 97.8% capacity with only 82 GB free, and looking at the session logs, it appears Veeam is deleting old restore points when free space drops below the 5% threshold. The logs show "Initiating merge of oldest restore point to reclaim space" each night, followed by "Restore point [date] removed." This seems like unexpected behavior — the job reports Success but is silently discarding restore points. I would have expected Veeam to either fail the job or at least flag a warning when it can't maintain the retention policy. The fact that it just silently removes restore points and reports green is misleading.

The repository is clearly too small for the configured retention. Either it needs to be expanded to hold 7 restore points, or the retention policy should be reduced to match what the repository can actually support. With 3 restore points consuming 3.92 TB of a 4 TB repository, there isn't room for a fourth without triggering one of these deletions.

I'd recommend expanding the repository capacity so the job can actually maintain its full retention window. The current configuration gives a false sense of security — seven green lights in the history but only three usable restore points.
