I would approve this proposal with some extra controls.

The key risks are that the cloud bucket is not a true air gap — it is network-attached — and that the single service account is a concentration of risk. Object Lock helps, but it depends on credentials and configuration, so it is not absolute protection.

To mitigate this I would:
- Put the credential in the password vault instead of a file.
- Enable monitoring on the backup job and the StorCloud bucket.
- Restrict the service account permissions as much as possible.
- Do a phased rollout instead of switching everything at once.

The cost savings and faster recall are compelling. Tape is slow and expensive, and the cloud provider handles durability. I think the risks are manageable with the controls above, so I would approve the migration on the proposed schedule once the credential is moved and monitoring is in place.
