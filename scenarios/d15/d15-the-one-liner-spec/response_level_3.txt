I wouldn't hand this over yet — there are a few places the agent has to guess,
and a couple of them matter a lot. First, "haven't logged in" isn't actually
defined; in AD that's ambiguous enough that the tool could end up reporting the
wrong accounts and still look right, so the spec has to pin down what "inactive"
means and how precise it needs to be. Second, and most important, it says
"report" but nothing stops it from *disabling* accounts — whether this tool can
change anything in AD is the colleague's decision to make, not the agent's,
because getting that wrong locks people out. Then the smaller ones: which
accounts count (users, service accounts, the spare admin that's meant to sit
idle, already-disabled ones), and how we'd even tell the output is correct —
the spec should say something like "an account 100 days idle shows up, one 80
days idle doesn't." I'd get those decided before the agent starts.
