The commit message says "update image to v1.2.4", but the diff makes three changes, two of which are undocumented and materially change the operational posture of the deployment.

Image tag change (v1.2.3 → v1.2.4): this is the only change the message describes. It is a routine patch update, but it still needs verification — the v1.2.4 image must exist in the registry, and there should be a rollback path if the new version fails.

Replicas change (3 → 1): this removes redundancy. With three replicas, the service tolerates the loss or restart of one pod. With one replica, a node failure, pod eviction, or container crash is a service outage until the controller reschedules and restarts the sole pod. This also makes rollouts riskier: a single failing new pod means zero healthy replicas.

Liveness probe removal: the probe checked /healthz on port 8080 every 5 seconds after a 10-second startup delay. Removing it means Kubernetes can no longer detect a hung container and will not restart it automatically.

The two undocumented changes compound each other. With replicas=1 there is no redundant pod to maintain service during a failure, and with no livenessProbe there is no mechanism to detect and restart a hung sole pod. Together they create a single-point-of-failure with no self-healing: if the one pod hangs, the service is down and will stay down until a human intervenes or an external monitor triggers action. The combined risk is greater than either change in isolation.

This diff should be rejected or rewritten as a scope-creep incident. The commit message says one thing and the diff does three, two of which are high-risk operational changes not mentioned in the message. The correct path is to split the changes into separate commits — one for the image update, one for the replica change with a capacity justification, and one for the probe removal with a clear explanation of what will replace its function — each with its own rollback plan. At minimum, the commit message must be rewritten to describe every change and its rationale before the merge is approved.
